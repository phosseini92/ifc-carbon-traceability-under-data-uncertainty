## Highlights

- 24/24 validation tests passed
- Independent manual carbon benchmark confirmed
- IFC4 schema validation completed with zero findings
- Seven fault-injection experiments verified
- Reproducibility audit completed after discovery and repair of a byte-level IFC fixture issue
- 45/45 generated output files reproduced byte-identically across independent full-pipeline runs

## Scope

This repository demonstrates traceable IFC-to-carbon screening workflows under data uncertainty. It is intentionally bounded and does not claim whole-building LCA validation, BEM calibration, general IFC interoperability, or semantic correctness of all material mappings.

## Research contribution

The demonstrator separates structural integrity, mapping integrity, and semantic validity to investigate which errors can be detected computationally and which require independent domain judgement.
